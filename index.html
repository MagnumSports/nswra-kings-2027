About the meeting

A championship with 150 meetings behind it

The NSWRA Open Championships have run since the nineteenth century. In June 2026 the Association marked its 150th meeting at Malabar. The 2027 meeting, the J G McIntosh Championships, opens the next 150, with the King's Prize as its centrepiece.

Individual

The King's Prize

Three stages across the week. The top shooters from the qualifying stages go through to the final, shot at long range under championship conditions.

Teams

State and club teams

Teams matches bring states, clubs and visiting teams onto the same firing points. Team entries are made online before the matches.

Open to all

Newcomers welcome

Target Rifle, F-Open, F-TR, F-Standard and SPC classes run in grades, so a first-time competitor shoots against people at their own level.

Venue

ANZAC Rifle Range, Malabar

The range has been in use since 1855 and is the home of the NSW Rifle Association. It sits on the Malabar Headland in Sydney's eastern suburbs, about 30 minutes from Sydney Airport and the CBD.

Getting there

Address
Franklin Street, Malabar NSW 2036
By car
Via Anzac Parade, then Franklin Street. Competitor parking is behind the firing points.
Public transport
Buses run along Anzac Parade to Malabar. Check Transport for NSW for current routes.
Flying in
Sydney Airport is about 12 km away.
